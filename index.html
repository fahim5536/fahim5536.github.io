About

A student by day,
a maker by habit.

I'm Tamzid — most people call me Fahim. I study at Gazipur Cantonment Public School and College, and I spend most of my free hours in a code editor or a design canvas.

Web development is where I solve problems: turning an idea into a fast, responsive, accessible page. Graphic design is where I slow down — typography, colour and the quiet details that make a brand feel like itself.

I'm still early in the journey, which means I'm curious, careful and genuinely excited about every project I get to work on. If you have something in mind, I'd love to hear about it.
