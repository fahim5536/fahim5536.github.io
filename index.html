About

A student by day,
a maker by habit.

I'm Tamzid Abdur Rahman — most people call me Fahim, and I also appear online as Tamzid Fahim or Fahim Tamzid. I'm an SSC 2028 batch student at Gazipur Cantonment Public School and College (GPCPSC) in Gazipur, Bangladesh, and I spend most of my free hours working on web development and graphic design.

On the web side, I build responsive, accessible interfaces with HTML, CSS and JavaScript. On the design side, I work on typography, layout and brand identity — calm visuals that feel like the people behind them. Whether you need a front-end developer in Gazipur, a web developer anywhere in Bangladesh, or a graphic designer for a small brand, I'd be glad to hear about it.
